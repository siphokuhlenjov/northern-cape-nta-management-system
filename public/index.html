Frequently asked questions

Find practical help before you apply.

Who can register?

Teachers can create an account to prepare applications. Eligibility and category requirements must be checked against the current official NTA guide.

How do I apply?

Create an account, sign in, start an application, complete the required fields, attach evidence, and submit before the published deadline.

Can I save my application?

Yes. Save a draft and return to it later. Check the status in your teacher workspace.

What documents can I upload?

The portal accepts PDF, JPG and PNG files up to the configured upload limit. The category guide determines which evidence is required.

What happens after submission?

Authorised district officials review submissions and may return an application for correction. Provincial staff can review compliance after district vetting.

How do I get support?

Use the support assistant at the bottom of the page for navigation and upload troubleshooting. It does not make award eligibility decisions.
