# Real data policy
Do not enter real personal information, identity numbers, SACE numbers, employment records or supporting documents until the system has been formally reviewed and approved. The local development version is not a substitute for the Department's approved records-management and security controls.

Never fabricate official schools, teacher records, scores, award outcomes or adjudication results. Seed data is limited to reference category and district names. Confirm all award-cycle rules, required documents, deadlines, eligibility and retention requirements against the official DBE guidance and departmental instructions for the relevant year.
